BusinessQuery reference

PA mit Option means private assets with option; PA ohne Option means private assets without option. BV mit Option means business assets with option; BV ohne Option means business assets without option; BV jur. Person means business assets for a legal person. Stiftung means foundation assets.

Legal entity mappings: natural person uses PVM and PVO; business uses BVM, BVO, and BVJ; Stiftung uses STI.

Supported tax fields are K40, K61, and K62.

Original/home currency is the currency of the fund or source tax data. EUR is the converted reporting value.

The FX rate and FX date show the conversion used to trace each original/home currency value into EUR.

Choose a specific tax year to filter results to that year. Choose All available years to query every available tax year.

If an ISIN has no data for the selected year, BusinessQuery shows: Data for ISIN {ISIN} is not available for the selected year.

The base value is the tax value before applying your entered amount. The calculated value is the base value multiplied by the amount multiplier.

Use Add New Query to enter ISINs, run a query, and save a reusable rule. Use Queries to load, edit, and group saved queries.

After loading a saved query, replace or paste the ISINs you want to review, then rerun the query.

CSV exports include the query name, ISIN, tax year, tax field, legal entity category, original/home currency values, EUR values, amount multiplier, FX rate, and FX date.

Search helps find available fund tax data by ISIN or fund/security name, then sends a selected ISIN into BusinessQuery.
